About QuakeTrace

QuakeTrace replays real USGS arrival times from the April 2024 Tewksbury, New Jersey earthquake sequence. A judge shifts one station's clock, and QuakeTrace finds which station is lying by refitting the epicentre without each station in turn. The station audit tracks the same stations across seven quakes to date when a clock broke.
